# Pulse - Intelligent Health & Vitality Tracker

Pulse is a modern, privacy-focused, zero-dependency health tracker application designed for daily wellness tracking, biometric monitoring, and long-term habit formation.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 💓 Daily Health Vitality Score
- **Dynamic Holistic Scoring (0–100)**: Evaluates 5 core pillars of wellness:
  1. Movement & Daily Steps
  2. Hydration Volume
  3. Sleep Duration & Architecture
  4. Caloric Balance & Macro Split
  5. Habit & Medication Adherence
- Visual status rings and real-time status indicators (*Optimal Vitality*, *Great Momentum*, *Fair Progress*, *Needs Attention*).

### 2. 👟 Activity & Workouts
- **Step Tracking**: Real-time progress bar, daily goals (default: 10,000 steps), and quick-add step buttons (`+1,000`, `+2,500`, `+5,000`).
- **Exercise Logging**: Categorized workouts (*Running, Cycling, Strength Training, HIIT, Yoga, Swimming, Pilates*), intensity levels, active durations, and estimated calorie burn.
- **7-Day Trend Chart**: Interactive canvas bar charts with custom tooltips.

### 3. 💧 Animated Hydration Station
- **Fluid Dynamics Visualizer**: A custom animated water bottle graphic that fills up with fluid as you log water throughout the day.
- **Quick Increments**: Single-click logging for 250ml (glass), 500ml (bottle), 750ml (flask), or 1,000ml (jug).
- **Time-stamped Ingestion History**: Review exact times and volumes of fluid intake with delete support.

### 4. 🥗 Nutrition & Macronutrient Budget
- **Calorie Tracker**: Remaining budget calculation against your daily energy goals.
- **Macro Split Breakdown**: Grams and ratios for **Protein**, **Carbohydrates**, and **Healthy Fats**.
- **Food Presets & Custom Logging**: One-click presets (*Avocado Toast & Eggs, Grilled Chicken & Rice, Salmon Bowl, Protein Shake*) plus full custom meal creation.

### 5. 🩺 Clinical Biometrics & Vitals
- **Blood Pressure Tracker**: Automatic categorization based on the **American Heart Association (AHA)** guidelines (*Optimal, Elevated, Stage 1 Hypertension, Stage 2 Hypertension*).
- **Heart Rate & Oxygen**: Resting HR (BPM) and blood oxygen ($SpO_2\%$).
- **Weight & BMI**: Dynamic Body Mass Index calculation with WHO health categories (*Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese*).
- **Dual-Trace BP & Weight Line Charts**: 14-day longitudinal trends.

### 6. 🌙 Sleep Architecture
- Bedtime and wake-up time tracking with duration calculation.
- Sleep quality ratings (*Restful, Average, Fragmented*) and overall Sleep Score (1–100).
- Sleep notes for evening habits, bedroom conditions, or sleep disruptions.

### 7. 🔥 Habits & Medications
- **Daily Checklist**: Track micro-habits (*Morning sunlight, Mindfulness meditation, Screen-free evenings*) with consecutive streak counters (🔥).
- **Medication & Supplement Schedule**: Daily prescription dosage reminders with checked-off state persistence.

### 8. 📊 Longitudinal Analytics
- 7-day and 14-day interactive Retina charts for steps, hydration, caloric burn, weight changes, and blood pressure.
- Native HTML5 Canvas rendering — zero external CDN or network dependencies required.

### 9. 🔒 Privacy & Data Portability
- **100% Local Storage**: No remote tracking, advertisements, or third-party cloud leaks. All metrics remain on your local machine.
- **Export & Import**: Download your entire health history as a single JSON file or import a backup anytime.
- **14-Day Demo Data Generator**: Instant one-click loader to preview realistic multi-week history and charts.

### 10. 🎵 Web Audio Sound Effects & Theme Engine
- Procedurally synthesized micro-sound cues (tap, water splash, success chime, celebration fanfare) generated via the browser's native **Web Audio API** (toggleable).
- **Dark Mode & Light Mode** with smooth transition aesthetics.

---

## 🚀 How to Run the App

### Option 1: Open Directly in Browser
Double-click `index.html` or open it with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox:



### Option 2: Install as a Desktop App (PWA)
1. Open `index.html` in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
2. In the browser address bar, click the **Install App** icon (or go to `Settings > Apps > Install Pulse Health Tracker`).
3. Launch Pulse directly from your Windows Start Menu or Taskbar like a native Windows application!
